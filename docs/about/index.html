The point of view

What counts as sleepy?

We do not discriminate here. The collection can make room for obvious naps, quiet corners, blanket energy, and cards making a very convincing case.

  • Actually asleep: no defense needed.
  • Sleep-adjacent: resting, lounging, dozing, or one yawn away.
  • Cozy by association: the scene has the right little feeling, even if nobody is technically napping.
