Cosmoem from Celebrations 14/25 is included in this guide as a sleepy Pokemon card for collectors building a calm, cozy, or nap-themed binder. As a rare card, it gives the sleepy Pokemon theme a little more collector weight without necessarily becoming the whole page. The card is illustrated by kirisAki, which is useful context for collectors who like arranging pages by artist as well as by Pokemon. Its low market price makes it one of the easiest sleepy Pokemon cards to add to a binder without stretching a collection budget. It works nicely in a Sword & Shield-era page, especially alongside Trainer Gallery and alternate-art sleepy cards.
