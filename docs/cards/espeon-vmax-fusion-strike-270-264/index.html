Espeon VMAX from Fusion Strike 270/264 is included in this guide as a sleepy Pokemon card for collectors building a calm, cozy, or nap-themed binder. As a Rare Rainbow, it is one of the showier entries in the sleepy Pokemon theme and can carry a page on its own. The card is illustrated by Kouki Saitou, which is useful context for collectors who like arranging pages by artist as well as by Pokemon. Its high market price puts it firmly in grail territory for a sleepy Pokemon collection, so it is best treated as a long-term chase card. It works nicely in a Sword & Shield-era page, especially alongside Trainer Gallery and alternate-art sleepy cards.
