Phanpy from Holon Phantoms 75/110 is included in this guide as a sleepy Pokemon card for collectors building a calm, cozy, or nap-themed binder. As a common card, it is useful for collectors who want the sleepy theme to feel complete beyond only the expensive hits. Its low market price makes it one of the easiest sleepy Pokemon cards to add to a binder without stretching a collection budget. It is also a good vintage-era contrast piece if you want a 3x3 page that mixes older sleepy cards with modern illustration rares.
