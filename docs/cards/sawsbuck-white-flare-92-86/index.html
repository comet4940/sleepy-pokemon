Caught napping

Sawsbuck

White Flare · 92/86 · Illustration Rare

Why it belongs. Sawsbuck has stretched out in a grassy meadow and fallen completely asleep, its leafy antlers blending right into the surrounding greenery. The whole scene feels wonderfully still and summery. Less “found a shady nap spot” and more “became part of the landscape and powered down.”
