Snorlax from Fusion Strike 206/264 is included in this guide as a sleepy Pokemon card for collectors building a calm, cozy, or nap-themed binder. As a common card, it is useful for collectors who want the sleepy theme to feel complete beyond only the expensive hits. The card is illustrated by Oswaldo KATO, which is useful context for collectors who like arranging pages by artist as well as by Pokemon. Its low market price makes it one of the easiest sleepy Pokemon cards to add to a binder without stretching a collection budget. It works nicely in a Sword & Shield-era page, especially alongside Trainer Gallery and alternate-art sleepy cards.
