Wigglytuff from Fates Collide 66/124 is included in this guide as a sleepy Pokemon card for collectors building a calm, cozy, or nap-themed binder. Its low market price makes it one of the easiest sleepy Pokemon cards to add to a binder without stretching a collection budget.
