Pikachu field notes · 5 cards

The Sleepiest of Pikachus

Every sleepy Pikachu currently in the collection, plus one extremely important cameo where Pikachu sneaks a nap into someone else’s card. Pikachu is always doing excellent work.

#1 / Lost Origin Trainer Gallery / TG05/TG30 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Pikachu

Pikachu / English / Atsushi Furusawa

Pikachu and Akari are both completely out under a tree. The centerpiece pick, and an extremely strong argument for taking the afternoon off.

#2 / ME: 30th Celebration / 027/128 / Pikachu Rare

Pikachu

Pikachu / English / Asako Ito

A tiny knitted Pikachu enjoying a tiny grassy nap. The attack is literally called Nap, so the research has been done for us.

#3 / ME: 30th Celebration / 043/128 / Pikachu Rare

Pikachu

Pikachu / English / OOYAMA

Pikachu fell asleep on a tropical beach and called it Tropical Vibes. Honestly, no notes.

#4 / Scarlet & Violet Promos / 214 / Promo

Pikachu

Pikachu / English / Kazuki Minami

Not asleep yet, but that eyes-shut, full-body yawn says the nap is minutes away. A fan painting that won the 2024 Illustration Contest, with an attack called Unwind.

#5 / Unified Minds / 88/236 / Common

Munna

Munna / English / Shibuzoh.

Munna is the star, but Pikachu is the one fast asleep, tucked under a flowery quilt with Eevee. Munna eats bad dreams, so this nap is in very good hands.
