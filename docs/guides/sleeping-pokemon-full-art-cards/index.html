Full art field notes · 15 cards

Sleepy Full Art Cards

On a full art card the nap takes up the whole card, with no frame around the art. Here are fifteen of our favorites: Sword & Shield alt arts, Trainer Gallery and Galarian Gallery cards, Special Illustration Rares, Illustration Rares, and one Chinese card worth tracking down.

#1 / Evolving Skies / 192/203 / Ultra Rare

Dragonite V

Dragonite / English / Atsushi Furusawa

If one card made full art and naps famous together, it's this one. Dragonite floats belly up across the whole card, eyes shut and smiling, while a flock of Spearow carries on without it.

#2 / Battle Styles / 155/163 / Ultra Rare

Tyranitar V

Tyranitar / English / HYOGONOSUKE

Tyranitar has eaten its way through a picnic and passed out on its back, mouth open and drooling, next to towers of empty plates. Check behind the tree: Pignite is peeking out, sweating, probably about the dishes.

#3 / Silver Tempest / 181/195 / Ultra Rare

Skuntank V

Skuntank / English / Jiro Sasumo

Look past the two Skuntank prowling the forest floor, all the way down to the bottom-left corner. A pair of tiny Stunky has curled up asleep beside a pile of fruit, the best nap on the card hiding where you'd look last.

#4 / Fusion Strike / 257/264 / Ultra Rare

Greedent V

Greedent / English / Saya Tsuruta

A whole row of Skwovet lines the log, cheeks stuffed and grinning at Greedent's berry feast. One in the front row has already closed its eyes. Spot it before the rest of the crowd joins in.

#5 / Fusion Strike / 270/264 / Rare Rainbow

Espeon VMAX

Espeon / English / Kouki Saitou

Dynamax makes a cat bigger, not more awake. Espeon drapes itself over a row of rooftops with its eyes closed, while birds circle the bright sky above.

#6 / Silver Tempest Trainer Gallery / TG09/TG30 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Druddigon

Druddigon / English / Teeziro

Trainer Gallery cards pair a Pokémon with its trainer, and these two make a good team: Ace Trainer Dara reads her map in the curl of Druddigon's body while Druddigon dozes with its head down.

#7 / Silver Tempest Trainer Gallery / TG07/TG30 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Rockruff

Rockruff / English / Hideki Ishikawa

Professor Kukui is down on the floor with a cloth, cleaning up. Rockruff is belly up on the rug, eyes shut and tongue out, contributing nothing.

#8 / Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery / GG15/GG70 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Solrock

Solrock / English / Nobuhiro Imagawa

Solrock's own flavor text says it gives off light like the sun when it spins. In this Galarian Gallery card the sun has set, the sky is turning purple, and Solrock has stopped spinning to doze in the flowers while Volbeat and Illumise take over the lighting.

#9 / Scarlet & Violet / 250/198 / Special Illustration Rare

Jacq

Jacq / English / Oswaldo KATO

A Special Illustration Rare for anyone who has fallen asleep studying. Jacq is face down at his desk with his glasses still on, a mug and a landslide of books and papers around him.

#10 / Temporal Forces / 209/162 / Special Illustration Rare

Bianca's Devotion

Bianca / English / Cona Nitanda

Bianca has crashed on the couch, curled into the cushions with her bag still on, and Musharna dozes right above her. All soft light and warm colors, it's one of the coziest Special Illustration Rares around.

#11 / Destined Rivals / 194/182 / Illustration Rare

Misty's Lapras

Lapras / English / Orca

Misty has dozed off against Lapras's shell, smiling, while Lapras gazes out to sea. A beach day for two, and only one of them is still awake.

#12 / Black Bolt / 148/086 / Illustration Rare

Pidove

Pidove / English / Mori Yuu

Count the Pidove: nearly every one has flopped over asleep on the cobblestones. The one in the middle is still staring straight at you.

#13 / Surging Sparks / 199/191 / Illustration Rare

Spheal

Spheal / English / miki kudo

A pod of Spheal dozing on the ice floes under a bright sun, except the one in the middle, rolling belly up with its eyes wide open. Its attack, Powder Snow, puts the opponent to sleep, so the others are covered.

#14 / Black Bolt / 097/086 / Illustration Rare

Darumaka

Darumaka / English / Tomowaka

Darumaka is said to fall asleep once the fire inside it dies down. This Illustration Rare catches that exact moment: sitting bolt upright on its cushion, eyes closed, not moving an inch.

#15 / Gem Pack Vol. 3 / 04 07/07 / Triple Rare

Cubone

Cubone / Simplified Chinese / Katsunori Sato

One for collectors who look beyond English sets: a Simplified Chinese Gem Pack card. Two Cubone sit together on a stone bridge at dusk under a street lamp, one leaning on the other with its eye closed, the gentlest scene on this list.
