Sleepy Cat Pokemon Cards

Every sleepy cat Pokemon card currently in the collection, researched with junior Pokemon researcher Linkcat: tiny kittens, dramatic big cats, psychic cats, electric cats, and a few cat-adjacent creatures making a very convincing case. We do not discriminate here.

#1 / Ascended Heroes / 165/217 / Common

Skitty

Skitty / English / Yoko Hishida

Skitty is a kitten curled up in a tiny bed. No debate, no notes, full marks for understanding the assignment.

#2 / Temporal Forces / 167/162 / Illustration Rare

Litten

Litten / English / Makura Tami

A literal Fire Cat enjoying a warm, very committed nap. Litten may be the purest expression of this guide.

#3 / Sun & Moon Promos / 259/SM-P / Promo

Litten

Books are flying, Chespin is mid-leap across the room, and Litten has curled up on the armchair with its face buried, ignoring all of it.
