Sleepy Pokemon Cameos

Sometimes the featured Pokemon is wide awake, but another sleeper quietly steals the scene. These cameos reward a closer look, and this growing shelf is here to help you spot them.

#1 / Black Bolt / 116/086 / Illustration Rare

Munna

Munna / English / Teeziro

Munna drifts beneath a crescent moon while two tiny Pidove sleep along the ledge below, easy to miss in Teeziro’s dreamy pink scene.

#2 / Unified Minds / 88/236 / Common

Munna

Munna / English / Shibuzoh.

Munna keeps watch from a pastel canopy bed while Pikachu and Eevee snooze beneath the floral quilt. The sweetest little bedroom cameos.

#3 / ME: 30th Celebration / 131/128 / Illustration Rare

Lapras

Lapras / English / Amelicart

Lapras drifts through a sunny lagoon, with Psyduck and its companions bringing a sleepy little cameo to the scene.

#4 / Wizards Promos / 49 / Promo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Craig Turvey

Snorlax is out cold under a comic-strip cloud of Zs, with Eevee tucked into the vintage snooze scene too.

#5 / Legendary Treasures / RC16/RC113 / Common

Ursaring

Ursaring / English / Sanosuke Sakuma

Ursaring has become a giant forest mattress for its sleeping cub, Teddiursa. Protective parent, perfect pillow.

#6 / Paradox Rift / 196/182 / Illustration Rare

Joltik

Joltik / English / Atsushi Furusawa

Joltik is wide awake on Yamper’s head, while the big dog has nodded off below. The real sleepy cameo is the one you might miss at first glance.

#7 / White Flare / 89/86 / Illustration Rare

Leavanny

Leavanny / English / mashu

Leavanny looks after a whole leafy nursery, and two of the Sewaddle have already nodded off in their hoods. The third, and a grumpy Swadloon, are holding out.

#8 / Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery / GG42/GG70 / Rare Holo VMAX

Zeraora VMAX

Zeraora / English / aoki

Pachirisu is snoozing on Zeraora’s forehead while the electric powerhouse stays right in the middle of all that thunder.

#9 / Collect 151: Hope / 171/151 / Art Rare

Pikachu

Pikachu / Simplified Chinese / Oswaldo KATO

Pikachu is having a perfectly nice day, but Psyduck has found a quiet spot by the water and is fully asleep. The little background nap is easy to miss on a first look.

#10 / Twilight Masquerade / 187/167 / Illustration Rare

Chansey

Chansey / English / Toshinao Aoki

Chansey is busy at the Pokemon Center while Snorlax has nodded off on the tiled floor. The whole room carries on around this absolutely unbothered snoozer.

#11 / Unbroken Bonds / 135/214 / Rare

Wigglytuff

Wigglytuff / English / miki kudo

Wigglytuff is wide awake in the sunny meadow, but the two Jigglypuff beside it are out cold. The supporting cast has made this a group nap, with Wigglytuff on pillow duty.

#12 / Neo Destiny / 48/105 / Uncommon

Light Jolteon

Jolteon / English / Naoyo Kimura

Jolteon keeps watch beneath the stars while Togepi sleeps against its chest and Rattata curls up nearby. A gentle guardian cameo for this shelf.

#13 / Stellar Crown / 172/142 / Special Illustration Rare

Lacey

Lacey / English / Souichirou Gunjima

The Blueberry League Elite Four are in the middle of something, and Drayton has quietly checked out. He’s easy to miss up in the top-right corner of the table, asleep on his arm.

#14 / HS—Triumphant / 89/102 / Uncommon

Twins

Mareep / English / Kanako Eo

The twins and their flower crowns fill the whole card, so the sleeper hides at the edge: Mareep, half out of frame, snoozing under a flower of its own.

#15 / Mysterious Treasures / 109/123 / Uncommon

Bebe's Search

Glameow / English / Ken Sugimori

Your eye goes straight to Bebe at the keyboard. Glameow, stretched out beside the laptop with its eyes shut, is the cat nap hiding in plain sight.

#16 / Sun & Moon Promos / 080/SM-P / Promo

Lillie

Cosmog / Japanese / Megumi Mizutani

Lillie and Clefairy are both awake. The sleeper is the small starry cloud between them: Cosmog has drifted off on the couch, sleep bubbles and all.

#17 / Fusion Strike / 257/264 / Ultra Rare

Greedent V

Greedent / English / Saya Tsuruta

Most of the Skwovet lined up along the log are still chewing and grinning. One near the middle has stopped: eyelids drooping and cheeks still full, it’s nodding off mid-snack while Greedent keeps eating.

#18 / Temporal Forces / 171/162 / Illustration Rare

Reuniclus

Reuniclus / English / Atsushi Furusawa

Reuniclus is having a splashy time in the shallows. Look up past it: a Duosion floats on its back across the top of the card, eyes closed, napping on the water.

#19 / Neo Destiny / 26/105 / Rare

Light Piloswine

Piloswine / English / Yukiko Baba

Count the Swinub: one asleep on top of Light Piloswine’s head and three more dozing against its shaggy sides. Piloswine has become a hill for a family nap.

#20 / Legendary Treasures / 107/113 / Uncommon

Bouffalant

Bouffalant / English / Sanosuke Sakuma

Two Natu have nested in Bouffalant’s afro. The one on the left is already asleep; its friend is keeping one eye open.

#21 / Neo Genesis / 100/111 / Common

Double Gust

Sentret / English / Tomokazu Komiya

A finger is choosing who gets switched in, Hoppip is sweating it out, and Sentret has simply lain down in the middle circle and fallen asleep.
