28 years of Snorlax naps · 22 cards

Snorlax, the Original Sleeper

Snorlax has been napping on Pokémon cards since 1998, and it has never once set an alarm. Here is every sleeping Snorlax in the collection, oldest to newest, plus one cameo where it nods off on the Pokémon Center floor.

#1 / Vending Machine Series 1 / 143 / Common

Snorlax

Snorlax / Japanese / Keita Takahashi

Where it all begins: a fan’s drawing that won CoroCoro’s 1998 contest, with Snorlax asleep in the middle of the road, exactly where its Pokédex says it might be.

#2 / Wizards Promos / 49 / Promo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Craig Turvey

The classic: out cold under the moon with comic-strip Z’s floating overhead. If you picture a sleeping Snorlax, you’re probably picturing this one.

#3 / Gym Heroes / 33/132 / Rare

Rocket's Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Shin-ichi Yoshida

Face-down among empty bottles and wrappers. Team Rocket’s Snorlax ate everything in reach and called it a night.

#4 / Skyridge / 100/144 / Common

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Naoyo Kimura

Up to its chin in a steaming hot spring, snowy mountains behind it. It is not getting out.

#5 / Kalos Starter Set / 26/39 / Common

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Naoki Saito

Face-down in a bed of white flowers, smiling. Possibly the gentlest nap in the collection.

#6 / Flashfire / 80/106 / Rare

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Mitsuhiro Arita

The original printing of the Generations sleeper: Mitsuhiro Arita’s Snorlax, flat out in the grass two years earlier.

#7 / Generations / 58/83 / Rare Holo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Mitsuhiro Arita

Just Snorlax, tall grass and nothing else. It dropped where it stood.

#8 / Fates Collide / 77/124 / Rare

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Tomokazu Komiya

The only Snorlax nap that causes a stampede: it rolls through town fast asleep, and its attack hits harder while it’s Asleep.

#9 / Unbroken Bonds / 158/214 / Rare Holo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / kanahei

Asleep under the trees with a Torchic on its head and two Pikachu nearby. Nobody is in a hurry to wake it.

#10 / Hidden Fates / 50/68 / Rare

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Akira Komayama

Arms flung wide, mid-snore, in the dappled shade of a big tree. The afternoon nap, perfected.

#11 / Sword & Shield Promos / SWSH032 / Promo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Tika Matsuno

A soft night sky and a cozy patch of ground. Classic bedtime.

#12 / Vivid Voltage / 131/185 / Rare Holo

Snorlax

Snorlax / English / Atsuko Nishida

Asleep on its side in the sunny grass, a paw tucked up by its face, in Atsuko Nishida’s warm storybook style.

#13 / Fusion Strike / 206/264 / Common

Snorlax

#14 / Pokémon GO / 55/78 / Rare Holo

Snorlax

Blocking a real-world entrance, just like in the game. Please walk around.

#15 / Lost Origin / 143/196 / Rare Holo

Snorlax

#16 / Crown Zenith / 109/159 / Rare

Snorlax
