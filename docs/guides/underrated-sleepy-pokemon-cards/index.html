On a budget · 12 cards

Underrated Sleepy Pokemon Cards

Some of the sleepiest art in the Pokémon TCG hides in commons, stadiums and overlooked rares that sell for a few dollars or less. Here are twelve cards that deserve more love, including a Special Illustration Rare and two Illustration Rares that cost less than a booster pack.

#1 / Obsidian Flames / 227/197 / Special Illustration Rare

Poppy

Poppy / English / Ryota Murayama

Special Illustration Rares aren’t supposed to cost this little. Poppy, the smallest of Paldea’s Elite Four, has fallen asleep against her enormous Copperajah, and the size difference is the whole joke.

#2 / Brilliant Stars Trainer Gallery / TG12/TG30 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Oranguru

Oranguru / English / Akira Komayama

Siesta goals. Sina sleeps in a hammock while Oranguru lounges on a pile of pillows with a fan, berries and cold drinks within reach. Easy to miss in a Trainer Gallery full of bigger names.

#3 / Journey Together / 166/159 / Illustration Rare

Lycanroc

Lycanroc / English / GIDORA

Everyone looks at the fierce Lycanroc standing guard. The sleeper is the Rockruff curled up in the bottom corner, and somehow nobody is chasing this Illustration Rare.

#4 / Phantasmal Flames / 104/94 / Illustration Rare

Togedemaru

Togedemaru / English / Orca

One dozes by the tree, the other peeks over the grass to see what it’s missing. Orca’s sunlit glade deserves more fans.

#5 / Celebrations / 14/25 / Rare

Cosmoem

Cosmoem / English / kirisAki

Who looks at a Cosmoem card twice? You should: Lillie and Cosmoem are fast asleep side by side, a Pokémon that weighs more than a ton and the girl who carried it around in a bag.

#6 / Unified Minds / 207/236 / Uncommon

Slumbering Forest

Slumbering Forest / English / Ryo Ueda

If this site had a home address, it would be this glowing mushroom grove. Stadium cards rarely make it into art binders. This one should open yours.

#7 / BREAKthrough / 18/162 / Common

Cyndaquil

Cyndaquil / English / Tomokazu Komiya

Fire-type. Firewood. Fast asleep on top of it. Tomokazu Komiya made a common that deserves a frame.

#8 / Unbroken Bonds / 137/214 / Uncommon

Togetic

Togetic / English / sui

Most people flip straight past uncommons. Flip back for this one: sui’s Togetic, asleep between pink blossoms in soft forest light.

#9 / Scarlet & Violet Promos / 14/215 / Promo

Koraidon

Koraidon / English / Mina Nakai

Scarlet’s legendary, snoozing on a cliff edge above a waterfall like it has nowhere to be. Promos rarely get a second look, and this one has earned several.

#10 / Cosmic Eclipse / 31/236 / Common

Tepig

Tepig / English / Mina Nakai

Face-down on the pillow, Poké Ball abandoned on the bed. Tepig did not plan this nap. Easy to lose in a 236-card set, and one of the most relatable cards here.

#11 / HS—Triumphant / 36/102 / Uncommon

Kangaskhan

Kangaskhan / English / Mitsuhiro Arita

Baby tucked into mom’s pouch, both of them dozing. One of Mitsuhiro Arita’s gentlest HeartGold & SoulSilver-era cards.

#12 / Legendary Treasures / 94/113 / Common

Gible

Gible / English / sui

Find the one who’s asleep. In a cave full of flustered, squabbling Gible, one at the top has quietly slipped off for a nap.
