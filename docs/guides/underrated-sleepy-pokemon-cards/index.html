On a budget · 12 cards

Underrated Sleepy Pokemon Cards

Some of the sleepiest art in the Pokémon TCG hides in commons, stadiums and overlooked rares that sell for a few dollars or less. Here are twelve cards that deserve more love, including a Special Illustration Rare and two Illustration Rares that cost less than a booster pack.

#1 / Obsidian Flames / 227/197 / Special Illustration Rare

Poppy

Poppy / English / Ryota Murayama

Special Illustration Rares usually cost a lot, and this one doesn’t. Poppy has fallen fast asleep against her enormous Copperajah, using the Steel-type giant as the world’s coziest pillow.

#2 / Brilliant Stars Trainer Gallery / TG12/TG30 / Trainer Gallery Rare Holo

Oranguru

Oranguru / English / Akira Komayama

Easy to miss in a Trainer Gallery full of bigger names: Sina asleep in a hammock while Oranguru lounges on a pile of pillows, fan in hand, surrounded by berries and cold drinks.

#3 / Journey Together / 166/159 / Illustration Rare

Lycanroc

Lycanroc / English / GIDORA

Nobody chases a Lycanroc Illustration Rare, which is their loss. A fierce wolf has collapsed in the dirt with limp paws after a long day of roughhousing.

#4 / Phantasmal Flames / 104/94 / Illustration Rare

Togedemaru

Togedemaru / English / Orca

Two round little Togedemaru fast asleep together in a sun-dappled glade. One of the cheapest full-art group naps, and Orca’s art deserves more fans.

#5 / Celebrations / 14/25 / Rare

Cosmoem

Cosmoem / English / kirisAki

Overlooked because it’s Cosmoem, of all Pokémon. Look closer: Lillie is asleep beside it, two companions sharing a perfectly ordinary nap.

#6 / Unified Minds / 207/236 / Uncommon

Slumbering Forest

Slumbering Forest / English / Ryo Ueda

Stadium cards get skipped by art collectors, but this one could be this site’s home address: a glowing mushroom grove made for sleeping.

#7 / Scarlet & Violet / 117/198 / Rare

Krookodile

Krookodile / English / Misa Tsutsui

A base-set rare most people trade away. Krookodile is propped against a palm on a moonlit beach, arms folded, a shooting star overhead, its mask doubling as sunglasses.

#8 / Paldea Evolved / 112/193 / Common

Makuhita

Makuhita / English / Kurata So

Look closely at the tree behind it: Makuhita punched dents into the bark during training, then knocked itself out upright under the full moon.

#9 / Lost Origin / 151/196 / Rare

Greedent

Greedent / English / sui

Sui’s Greedent, passed out after a berry feast with crumbs still on its belly. The best food coma in the collection, in a set remembered for its chase cards.

#10 / Cosmic Eclipse / 31/236 / Common

Tepig

Tepig / English / Mina Nakai

A common lost in a 236-card set: Tepig crashed face-down on a pillow, Poké Ball left beside it, a tiny Fire-type who simply ran out of energy.

#11 / HS—Triumphant / 36/102 / Uncommon

Kangaskhan

Kangaskhan / English / Mitsuhiro Arita

Mitsuhiro Arita’s family nap, with the baby tucked into mom’s pouch. A HeartGold & SoulSilver-era Arita that still sells for about a dollar.

#12 / Legendary Treasures / 94/113 / Common

Gible

Gible / English / sui

Spot the sleeper: in a cave full of flustered, squabbling Gible, one has quietly slipped away for a nap at the top. A sui card few people notice.
